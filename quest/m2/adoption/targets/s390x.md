# [M] rind on s390x

## Goal

rind and rind-rustls build for s390x-unknown-linux-gnu on the [portable backend](/ARCHITECTURE.md), and CI runs the test suite (Wycheproof, differential, rind-rustls handshakes) under qemu on each.

## Plan

Big-endian. s390x's CPACF instructions are out of scope until a consumer measures a need. A missing CPU feature still fails with an error naming it; the portable backend needs none. Add the targets to [PLATFORMS.md](/PLATFORMS.md) and the release checks in the same PR.
