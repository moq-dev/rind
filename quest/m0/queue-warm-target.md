# [M] The merge queue builds through mbx, warm across groups

## Goal

Each merge group's `just ci` on the `rind-queue` runner reuses the compiled work of the groups before it instead of rebuilding from an empty `target/`, from a store whose size the host already bounds. The runner's writes per job fall measurably, and a group's result never depends on files a previous group left in the checkout.

## Plan

Measured on the NixOS host on 2026-11-13 in [kixel-ai/dotfiles#135](https://github.com/kixel-ai/dotfiles/pull/135): rind-ci wrote about 155 GB in 13.5 hours (about 275 GB a day, 5.7 GB a job over 27 jobs), the host's third-largest writer on a drive that wears out in months. 65% of its traced writes were `target/debug/deps`, 16% build-script output (aws-lc-sys under several hashes) and 12% incremental state, and the running job's `target/` was born at its start and reached 11 GB. The cause: `actions/checkout` defaults to `clean: true`, whose `git clean -ffdx` deletes the gitignored `target/` inside the checkout before every job. Dotfiles' `CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0` for the runners removes the incremental part (about 35 GB a day); this quest removes the rest. Expected: rind-ci from about 240 to about 50 GB a day.

- Decided 2026-11-13 (maintainer, /quest-plan): ✅ m0 (rejected: m1). m0 covers work that starves the agent hosts, and this is nixos's drive wear.
- ✅ Build through mbx, as kixel-ai/rust2's `queue-build.yml` and moq-dev/moq's `.github/actions/rust-cache` do (rejected: a `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` outside the checkout; `clean: false`). The host already sets the runners' `MBX_TARGET_ROOT` outside the checkout, bounds it (`MBX_TARGET_MAX_SIZE`, `MBX_GC_MAX_TOTAL_SIZE`, 7-day ages) and collects it at job boundaries (dotfiles' `nixos/modules/ci-runners.nix`), and mbx keys each compilation on its compiler, target and inputs. A plain target dir outside the checkout stays warm but nothing collects it, so it grows with every toolchain and lockfile change until someone writes a sweep. `clean: false` has the same growth and also leaves the previous group's untracked files in the tree, where a build could read them.
- Copy rust2's steps into `queue-build.yml` inline, since its header forbids local actions: `jdx/mr-boxington-action` pinned by SHA with `backend: local`, the guard that only main's merge queue writes the shared store, dropping another mbx version's private state under `$RUNNER_WORKSPACE`, and `mbx setup` with the check that the shim exists. Pin a version the host's fixers already carry (dotfiles' `mbxQueue`: 1.29.2 or 1.30.0); 1.30.0, moq's, is the newer.
- The dev shell puts the shim ahead of its pinned toolchain when the host has one, as rust2's `flake.nix` `shellHook` does. `just ci` also enters `nix develop .#msrv`, `.#aarch64`, `.#armv7`, `.#ct-vartime` and `.#dart` from `sh/dispatch.sh`, each with its own toolchain on `PATH`; every shell the queue's `just ci` builds in needs the hook, or that part stays cold. Decided 2026-11-13 (maintainer): ✅ the hook acts on any host with a shim, agents' included, as in rust2 (rejected: CI only). That is also what [mbx-dev-shell](/quest/m2/mbx-dev-shell.md) plans, so this quest lands its `shellHook`.
- Explicit target dirs bypass mbx's managed target: `check:fixtures`' `--target-dir target` in `sh/dispatch.sh`, and any other `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` or `--target-dir` the queue's `just ci` reaches. Find each and drop the override where mbx keeps the builds apart itself; keep one only with a stated reason.
- `queue-build.yml` runs main's copy, so the change first runs on the group after it lands. Before review, build twice through the shim from two fresh checkouts with a scratch `MBX_CACHE_DIR` and `MBX_TARGET_ROOT`, and record the second build's `mbx stats` hits and bytes written. After it lands, compare the next day's rind-ci writes with the baseline above from the container's `Consumed ... written to disk` journal lines, and note the result on the PR.
- The rind land fixer starts warm only when dotfiles' `nixos/modules/quest-land.nix` gives rind `fixerCaches.mbx` with the version pinned here (today rind takes only `cargo`). That change is dotfiles' to plan; list it in this PR's follow-ups for `/quest-plan` there.
