# [XL] ML-KEM-768's construction is proven

## Goal

ML-KEM-768 key generation, encapsulation, and decapsulation earn the ✓ on aarch64 and on x86_64 with AVX2 (without the AVX-512 sampler, which [its own quest](/quest/m4/verified/mlkem-avx512-sampler.md) adds): rind's FIPS 203 construction is proven down to s2n-bignum's and mlkem-native's proven polynomial routines and the proven sponge, whose theorems (`shake128x4_squeeze_spec`, `shake256_batch_spec`, `squeezing_squeeze_spec`, and the SHA-3 contexts') `proofs/Rind/X86_64/Sha3.lean` states. rind-rustls's default key exchange, X25519MLKEM768, then earns it wherever X25519 has it.

## Plan

- Runs: `mid/mlkem.rs` and `mid/mlkem/sample_ntt.rs` (about 1,300 lines: K-PKE, the Fujisaki-Okamoto transform with its constant-time compare and select, CBD sampling, SampleNTT's driver, encapsulation-key checks), `low/generic/mlkem.rs`, and the Rust byte encoding and decompression on aarch64 (frombytes, compress), over s2n-bignum's NTT, inverse NTT, base multiplication, mulcache, reduce, tomont, tobytes, frombytes, unpack, and rejection sampling, and on x86_64 mlkem-native's AVX2 compression, all proven in HOL Light. The construction is generic over ML-KEM-512, 768, and 1024, so its proof covers all three.
- Start from: `proofs/Rind/SymCrypt/MlKem.lean` vendors SymCrypt's FIPS 203 spec, `proofs/Rind/MlKem.lean` proves its `NTT` and `NTTInv` in closed form, and `proofs/Rind/S2nMlKem.lean` transliterates the specs the NTT, inverse NTT, mulcache, base multiplication, and tomont theorems state on both targets and proves each is FIPS 203's `NTT`, `NTTInv`, or `MultiplyNTTs`, in Montgomery form and the AVX2 order where the routine keeps them.
- Bridges left: tobytes and frombytes to `ByteEncode 12` and `ByteDecode`, x86_64's unpack to `avx2Layout`, rejection sampling to SampleNTT's loop, and mlkem-native's compression to `Compress`.
- Axioms: restate each routine's HOL Light theorem over its Rust wrapper under the composition rule, as `Rind/<Arch>/S2nBignum.lean` does. The constant tables (zetas) the theorems assume are rind's; prove them as `Rind/Aarch64/P256Table.lean` proves P-256's generator table.
- Translation: Aeneas rejects much of the ML-KEM Rust as written: `zip` iterators, closures, an `impl Iterator` of `&mut` rows, and function pointers. Restructure it, benchmarked against aws-lc-rs, or model it by hand as the SHA-3 dispatch is.
- Proofs: K-PKE and the Fujisaki-Okamoto transform over the translation, CBD sampling, SampleNTT's driver, compression and decompression, and the encapsulation-key checks, composed to SymCrypt's `KeyGen_internal`, `Encaps_internal`, and `Decaps_internal`, with `Encaps.KeyCheck` and `Decaps.KeyCheck`. SymCrypt's own proofs ([README-VERIFIEDCRYPTO.md](https://github.com/microsoft/SymCrypt/blob/c2e575ace0ea4b6b7a4184c1f19b81d1d5b2b5be/README-VERIFIEDCRYPTO.md), about 38,000 lines for 2,125 of Rust) cover its Rust, not rind's; reuse its encoding and compression lemmas where their shape fits.
