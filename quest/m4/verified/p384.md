# [L] P-384 ECDH and ECDSA are proven on x86_64 and aarch64

## Goal

P-384 key agreement and ECDSA earn the ✓ on x86_64 and aarch64, from the agreement and signature APIs to s2n-bignum's routines, as P-256's do on aarch64.

## Plan

- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#628](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/628): P-384's group law over a curve-generic NIST layer (`Rind/Nist.lean`, `Rind/NistGroup.lean`), Pratt certificates for p and n with G's order (`Rind/P384Group.lean`, `Rind/Pratt.lean`), s2n-bignum's contracts for the field routines and `p384_montjscalarmul` on both targets, and `p384_agree` in `Rind/X86_64/P384.lean`, which `admin/prove` copies to aarch64: agreement with a held key, from decoding through d·Q and the conversion to affine.
- ✅ [kixel-ai/rind#628](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/628) lands as a partial step (rejected: keep it open until the comb is proven), settled by the maintainer on 2026-11-11: the agreement proof stands on its own, the Rust comb proof is a new kind of proof, and both together are past one PR's review size.
- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#654](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/654): k·G and key generation on both targets. `Rind/P384Comb.lean` proves the Booth windows' sum and that no comb addition meets equal points below n, `Rind/X86_64/P384Table.lean` has the kernel check the 20 tables, `Rind/X86_64/P384Dispatch.lean` models the draws, and `Rind/X86_64/P384Keygen.lean` proves `p384_generate`, `p384_from_scalar`, and `p384_public_key`, with s2n-bignum's contracts for `p384_montjdouble`, `p384_montjmixadd`, `bignum_neg_p384`, `bignum_mod_n384`, and `bignum_mux`. They take the affine table select as the hypothesis `AffSelect`.
- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#746](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/746): the affine table select on both targets, which discharges `AffSelect`, so P-384 ECDH has its ✓. The NEON select is hand-modelled in `Rind/Aarch64/PointSelectModel.lean` over four new NEON intrinsic models, and `Rind/RowSelect.lean` holds what both targets' select proofs share. Groundwork for ECDSA: `admin/prove` translates P-384's ECDSA (`Curve`, `PrivateKey`, `PublicKey`, and `Scalar` for P-384, `SignatureHash` for SHA-384), with `p384_montjadd` and the P-384 `generate_random_key` opaque in each `FunsExternal.lean`; `Rind/P384Multiples.lean` has P-384's group laws, twin multiples, and `p384_order`; and `Rind/X86_64/Naf.lean` proves `public_naf` for any number of words, which P-256's verification now uses.

- Remaining: verification's wNAF double multiplication (`public_twin_multiply`, 385 digits), and the signing and verification glue, `P384Ecdsa.lean` and `P384Signature.lean` beside P-256's, with `EcdsaDispatch.lean` modelling `ECDSA_P384_SHA384_FIXED`. P-384's field and point lemmas (`jrep`, `feNat`, `pairNat6`) live in `P384.lean` and `P384Keygen.lean` over `nist.Point`, not P-256's `dec` form, so P-256's ECDSA proof does not carry over by renaming alone.

- Decided 2026-11-12 (maintainer, /quest-unblock): ✅ discharge `AffSelect` by hand-modelling `select_avx2`/`select_sse2` and the NEON select, quoting their Rust, since their raw-pointer loads do not translate, with new intrinsic models tested by `admin/simd-vectors.rs` as `Rind/X86_64/Simd.lean`'s are (rejected: replace them with s2n-bignum's `bignum_copy_row_from_table`). - ECDSA: reuse P-256's structure (`P256Multiples.lean`, `P256Ecdsa.lean`, `P256Signature.lean`) over `Rind/Spec.lean`'s `nist.ecdsaSign` and `nist.ecdsaVerifies`, which already take the curve's p, n, and G, and the keygen and comb from [kixel-ai/rind#654](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/654).
