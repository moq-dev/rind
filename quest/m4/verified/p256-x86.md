# [L] P-256 ECDH and ECDSA are proven on x86_64

## Goal

P-256 key agreement, ECDSA signing, and ECDSA verification, which every TLS certificate chain with an ECDSA key exercises, earn the ✓ on x86_64, as they do on aarch64.

## Plan

- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#648](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/648): P-256 agreement, `p256_agree` in `Rind/X86_64/P256Agree.lean`, down to s2n-bignum's `p256_scalarmul` and its `_alt` form.
- Done in [kixel-ai/rind#706](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/706): k·G runs rind's Rust select (`bignum_aff_point_select_p256`, `select_avx2::<8, 2>` and `select_sse2::<8, 4>`), which `Rind/X86_64/PointSelect.lean` proves through a hand model and intrinsic models tested by `admin/simd-vectors.rs`, generically in the row width, so P-384 and P-521 can use it too. `Rind/X86_64/P256Base.lean` proves rind's Booth-7 driver computes k·G for k below n, over `Rind/P256Booth.lean`'s recoding sum and no-doubling argument and `Rind/X86_64/P256Table.lean`'s kernel check of the 37-row table. Key generation and ECDSA's proofs (`P256.lean`, `P256Ecdsa.lean`, `P256Signature.lean`, `EcdsaDispatch.lean`) moved to `Rind/X86_64/`, which `admin/prove` copies to aarch64, over each target's `Rind/<Arch>/P256Base.lean` and `P256KeyCheck.lean`.
- Decided 2026-11-12 (maintainer, /quest-unblock): ✅ k·G runs rind's Rust select, `bignum_point_select`'s `select_avx2::<8, 2>` and `select_sse2::<8, 4>`, which P-384 and P-521 share and Lean proves through intrinsic models, accepting about 2.4% on key generation and nothing on signing (rejected: keep aws-lc's `select_w7` and `avx2_select_w7` and prove them in HOL Light after extending s2n-bignum's model with PCMPEQD, VPCMPEQD, VPERMD, VMOVD, and PREFETCHT0). One select proof serves all three curves, and fewer model extensions enter the trust base.
- Decided 2026-11-13 (maintainer, /quest-complete): ✅ land [kixel-ai/rind#706](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/706) as a partial step, with aws-lc's mixed addition as the placeholder axiom `p256_point_add_affine_spec`, no ✓ claimed until the HOL Light proof replaces it (rejected: keep that PR open until the HOL Light proof lands; thread the addition through every shared P-256 theorem as a hypothesis, as `OnePass` does, which the proof would remove again).
- Remaining: aws-lc's mixed addition, `ecp_nistz256_point_add_affine` and its BMI2 and ADX form `_affinex`, is the placeholder axiom `p256_point_add_affine_spec` in `Rind/X86_64/S2nBignum.lean`, so x86_64's P-256 theorems rest on it and earn no ✓. Prove it in HOL Light on s2n-bignum's x86_64 model, in rind's fork `thirdparty/s2n-bignum`, against its P-256 specs (`represents_p256`, `represents2_p256`, `group_mul p256_group`), covering the three cases the axiom states (p2 all zeros gives p1; p1's z zero gives (x2, y2, 2^256 mod p); otherwise the sum, for unequal points), for the in-place call, as `proofs/hol-light/x86_64/ecp_nistz256_point_add_affine.ml`, registered in `admin/prove`'s `hol_light_proofs`. Then restate it as the axiom's citation, and add x86_64's `p256-ecdh`, `p256-ecdsa-sign`, and `p256-ecdsa-verify` rows to `benchmarks/verified.toml`.
