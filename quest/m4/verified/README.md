# Every rind path is proven, with the smallest trust base

## Goal

Every operation rind exports earns `benchmarks/verified.toml`'s strict ✓ on every hot tier of x86_64 and aarch64 (the cold tiers are [m5's](/quest/m5/verified/README.md)), including what the [m3 verified epic](/quest/m3/verified/README.md) accepted as a gap, and every algorithm m4 adds. What those proofs trust shrinks to the published theorems, Lean, and Aeneas.

## Plan

Decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-07, planning m4's long-term work with no token budget. This reopens the m3 audit's "accepted unless a route changes it" list: VAES AES-GCM, SIMD ChaCha20-Poly1305, SHA-256 and SHA-1 on every tier, RSA end to end, ML-KEM's construction, P-384 and P-521 glue, x86_64 P-256, armv7's curves, the AES and GHASH fallbacks, and the rest.

- Prove the fast code rind runs; never swap in slower verified code to earn a ✓. Where no proof of a routine exists, write one in HOL Light, in [`proofs/hol-light/`](/proofs/README.md#hol-light-proofs), or in Lean. Offering it upstream is optional, with no standing approval: each quest stops and asks the maintainer before posting to awslabs/s2n-bignum or anywhere outside [the orgs AGENTS.md lists](/AGENTS.md#development) (decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-07 on [kixel-ai/rind#448](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/448)).
- The m3 epic's settled choices hold: Aeneas to Lean for rind's Rust, and its composition rule.
- Staging (maintainer, 2026-11-09, after an audit found most children are person-months of HOL Light with no measured cost and nothing ordering them): ✅ calibrate first, and move the cold tiers to m5 (rejected: calibrate only, leave this plan as written). The aarch64 SHA-256 proof moved to m2 as the calibration and landed in [kixel-ai/rind#552](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/552), whose body proposes a size for each child here that writes new HOL Light. armv7, MD5, SHA-1, and the tiers without AES or SHA hardware moved to [m5](/quest/m5/verified/README.md).
- Audit ([kixel-ai/rind#448](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/448), 2026-11-06), counting [the m3 epic](/quest/m3/verified/README.md)'s open quests as done: the ✓ then holds for X25519 and ChaCha20 header protection on x86_64 and aarch64, P-256 on aarch64, AES-GCM on x86_64's Vale tier, SHA-256 and its MACs and KDFs on x86_64 with SHA-NI, and SHA-384/512 and Ed25519 on Intel x86_64 with AVX but not AVX2 and on aarch64 without the SHA-512 instructions. Every other cell is a child below, ranked by how often the operation and tier run.
  - Upstream since m3: s2n-bignum proves aarch64 AES-GCM against SP 800-38D, out of place only, and models the aarch64 SHA-512 instructions; its FIPS 180-4 specs and x86_64 SHA-NI proof ([awslabs/s2n-bignum#415](https://github.com/awslabs/s2n-bignum/pull/415), [#420](https://github.com/awslabs/s2n-bignum/pull/420), [#450](https://github.com/awslabs/s2n-bignum/pull/450)) are still open. No proof covers the ChaCha20-Poly1305 or VAES AES-GCM assembly rind runs, or any aarch64 ChaCha20 or Poly1305; aws-lc-verification and HACL\* have added nothing rind runs.
  - Most children write HOL Light for assembly no one has proven. Decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-07 on [kixel-ai/rind#448](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/448): those proofs live in [`proofs/hol-light/`](/proofs/README.md#hol-light-proofs), run by `admin/prove` beside the Lean proofs over a pinned or vendored s2n-bignum, not in a fork. The SHA-2 children may pin [awslabs/s2n-bignum#415](https://github.com/awslabs/s2n-bignum/pull/415)'s and [#420](https://github.com/awslabs/s2n-bignum/pull/420)'s unmerged specs by commit, moving to the merged versions when they land, with the ✓ noting the pin.
  - An instruction a child needs that s2n-bignum's model lacks goes into rind's own extension of the model, in the x86 model files of rind's fork, `thirdparty/s2n-bignum`, for x86_64, with random-instruction cosimulation against the CPU in its `x86/proofs/rind_cosim.ml`, and is listed in [its `RIND.md`](/thirdparty/s2n-bignum/RIND.md) so it can be offered upstream later (maintainer, 2026-11-11, on [kixel-ai/rind#627](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/627); rejected: post to awslabs/s2n-bignum first, or wait for upstream's open pull requests). That `RIND.md` lists every change rind's fork makes to s2n-bignum.
  - pyca/verified-garbage (VG) proves its own assembly in Lean for nearly every cell still open here, but not the code rind runs. Decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-12: [the m2 VG epic](/quest/m2/verified-garbage/README.md) measures it and switches rind to it wherever it is fastest, and the unstarted children whose code it may replace (ChaCha20-Poly1305, P-521, RSA, and ML-KEM's AVX-512 sampler and portable arithmetic) wait on [that measurement](/quest/m2/verified-garbage/bench.md).
  - Parsing and randomness stay outside the ✓, as `benchmarks/verified.toml` defines it.
  - No algorithm from [post-quantum](/quest/m4/pq/README.md) or [protocols](/quest/m4/protocols/README.md) had landed then. ML-DSA, ristretto255, hash-to-curve, blind RSA, and ECVRF have since (2026-11-12), none with a proof route planned. Decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-07 on [kixel-ai/rind#448](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/448): [a recurring re-audit](/quest/m4/verified/re-audit.md) at the end of each milestone covers them as they land, rather than each algorithm's quest stating its own route.
